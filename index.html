Tips
  • Press Start listening before the song begins. It usually locks on within a few seconds of the music starting.
  • Wear the earbud in one ear only, so you still hear the room and your own voice.
  • If it loses the thread, it quietens your part and finds its place again by itself.
  • Keep the screen on and this page in front. Phones stop listening when the screen locks.
  • If the song has several identical choruses, it may land in an earlier one. The backing sounds the same, so you will still be in step.
